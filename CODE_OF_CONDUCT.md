# Code of Conduct

We want this course to be a welcoming, rigorous and kind place to learn and contribute, for people of every background, region, language, gender, ability, career stage and level of experience.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Ask questions without fear of ridicule; there are no "stupid" questions in a course whose purpose is learning.
- Give feedback that is specific, kind and about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and contributions.
- Respect privacy: never share personal, patient or participant data, or other people's private messages.
- Discuss sensitive topics (ancestry, disease, equity, history of research abuses) with care and accuracy, centring the people affected.
- Accept that expertise differs: share what you know and invite correction.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, intimidation, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Demeaning or stereotyping remarks about people or regions, including claims of biological superiority or inferiority of populations.
- Posting private information of others, or real patient or participant data.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews; promotion unrelated to the course.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact in the repository's **Security → Policy** page or the email address in `contribute/maintainers.md` (add yours when you launch). Reports are handled confidentially and fairly; retaliation against reporters is not tolerated.

## Scope and attribution

This Code applies in all project spaces (repository, discussions, live sessions) and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the widely used Contributor Covenant (version 2.1, https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is available under CC BY 4.0.
