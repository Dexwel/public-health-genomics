# Master resource list

These are the standard, trusted resources the course leans on. Links go to the organisations' main pages (which are stable); search their sites for the specific guideline, tutorial or database named. Each week's **Go deeper** tab narrows this list to what you need that week. *Found a better resource or a dead link? Please [tell us](../contribute/README.md).*

## Learn the tools

| Resource | What for | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| [The Carpentries](https://carpentries.org) | Beginner-friendly, community-taught lessons: *The Unix Shell*, *Version Control with Git*, *R for Reproducible Scientific Analysis*, and *Data Carpentry Genomics* | 2, 3, 5, 6 |
| [R for Data Science (2e)](https://r4ds.hadley.nz) | The best free book for data wrangling and plotting in R | 3 |
| [Galaxy Training Network](https://training.galaxyproject.org) | Hundreds of free tutorials on genomics analysis, many with a point-and-click option | 5-8, 10 |
| [EMBL-EBI Training](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training) | Free online courses on sequences, variants, databases, and ontologies | 5-8 |
| [HTSlib / samtools / bcftools documentation](http://www.htslib.org/doc/) | The authoritative manuals for SAM/BAM/VCF tools | 6, 7 |
| [PLINK 1.9 documentation](https://www.cog-genomics.org/plink/1.9/) | Every PLINK command used here | 4, 8, 9 |
| [Bioconductor](https://www.bioconductor.org) | R packages and workflows for genomic data | 3, 8 |

## Classic papers to look up by title

These are widely cited foundations; search for them by title (and check the current version of any guideline).

- Wilson JMG, Jungner G. *Principles and practice of screening for disease.* WHO Public Health Papers 34, 1968.
- Richards S, et al. *Standards and guidelines for the interpretation of sequence variants: a joint consensus recommendation of the ACMG and AMP.* Genetics in Medicine, 2015.
- Tavtigian SV, et al. *Modeling the ACMG/AMP variant classification guidelines as a Bayesian classification framework.* Genetics in Medicine, 2018.
- Marees AT, et al. *A tutorial on conducting genome-wide association studies: Quality control and statistical analysis.* International Journal of Methods in Psychiatric Research, 2018.
- Uffelmann E, et al. *Genome-wide association studies.* Nature Reviews Methods Primers, 2021.
- Choi SW, Mak TS-H, O'Reilly PF. *Tutorial: a guide to performing polygenic risk score analyses.* Nature Protocols, 2020.
- Martin AR, et al. *Clinical use of current polygenic risk scores may exacerbate health disparities.* Nature Genetics, 2019.
